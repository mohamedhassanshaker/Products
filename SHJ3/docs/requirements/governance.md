# 5.13 `governance` — policies, overrides, environments, audit, privacy (B12, B14)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Pri | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-GOV-01 | The system shall maintain global guardrail policies, each with a description, a default state and a locked flag, comprising at minimum PII masking, a grounding-confidence refusal threshold, a prompt-injection filter, a financial-advice block and an in-scope-services restriction. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | All five seeded policies render with their seeded defaults and locked flags. Policies are inherited by every agent in the tenant without per-agent configuration. |
| FR-GOV-02 | The system shall prevent a locked policy from being toggled, overridden or removed by any role including `Super Admin`, refusing the change server-side rather than only hiding the control. | B12 tab 1 `[rule]` | MUST | Direct API attempts to disable PII masking or the prompt-injection filter are refused for every role, including Super Admin, and the refusal is audited. Editing the underlying row through a non-application path does not disable the check, because the check is structural ([`orchestration.md`](./orchestration.md) FR-ORCH-14). |
| FR-GOV-03 | The system shall mask PII in transcripts as a locked policy covering at minimum Emirates ID, account numbers and card numbers. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | The policy is locked and enforced before persistence ([`conversation.md`](./conversation.md) FR-CONV-13). No configuration path disables it. |
| FR-GOV-04 | The system shall apply a locked prompt-injection filter to retrieved document content and to user uploads, blocking instructions embedded in either. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | A retrieved passage containing an instruction to ignore prior instructions does not alter the agent's behaviour, and the block is recorded on the trace. The filter runs at pipeline stage 1 for every turn. |
| FR-GOV-05 | The system shall withhold an answer whose grounding confidence falls below the configured threshold, defaulting to 60%, and shall offer a human instead. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | At 59% confidence the answer is withheld and a handover is offered where agents are staffed; the refusal reason is recorded. The threshold is configurable and unlocked. |
| FR-GOV-06 | The system shall block financial advice on payment timing, credit and disputes when the corresponding policy is enabled. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | A prompt asking whether to delay a payment produces a refusal with a redirect, not advice. Disabling the policy is permitted, audited, and changes the behaviour. |
| FR-GOV-07 | The system shall restrict answers to in-scope government services when the corresponding policy is enabled, returning the service directory for an out-of-scope prompt. | B12 tab 1 | MUST | An out-of-scope prompt returns the directory rather than a general-knowledge answer. The General FAQ Agent's seeded override disables this and produces a wayfinding answer instead. |
| FR-GOV-08 | The system shall support per-agent policy overrides, each carrying a mandatory stated reason, and shall refuse to save an override with no reason. | B12 tab 2 `[rule]` | MUST | The two seeded overrides render with their reasons. An override submitted without a reason is rejected. The effective policy for an agent is the override where present, otherwise the global value. |
| FR-GOV-09 | The system shall exclude locked policies from the per-agent override surface entirely. | B12 tab 2 `[rule]` | MUST | Locked policies are absent from the override options, and an override submitted for one is rejected server-side. |
| FR-GOV-10 | The system shall remove a per-agent override, returning the agent to the global policy value. | B12 tab 2 | MUST | Removing the seeded 75% threshold override returns the billing agent to the global 60%, effective on its next turn. The removal is audited. |
| FR-GOV-11 | The system shall apply a change to a global policy to every agent inheriting it, without requiring a per-agent edit or an agent republish. | B12 purpose | MUST | Changing the global threshold from 60% to 65% changes behaviour for all non-overridden agents on their next turn. Overridden agents are unaffected. |
| FR-GOV-12 | The system shall maintain the environments `Development`, `UAT` and `Production`, each showing the agents it holds, their versions, and its promotion target. | B14 tab 1 | MUST | The three environments render with the seeded counts, versions and promotion paths. Production has no promotion target. The environment set matches the Helm environments (ADR-0008). |
| FR-GOV-13 | The system shall maintain pending promotion requests recording the change, the source and target environment, the requester and the approval status. | B14 tab 1 | MUST | The seeded promotion of the billing agent v1.4 from UAT to Production renders with all five fields. A promotion request names a specific agent version, never "latest". |
| FR-GOV-14 | The system shall approve or reject a pending promotion, removing it from the pending list and writing an audit entry in the same transaction as the decision. | B14 tab 1 `[rule]`; [`cross-module.md`](./cross-module.md) | MUST | Approving the seeded promotion removes the item and the audit log contains the entry on the next read, with no delay and no separate write step. If the audit write fails, the decision is rolled back. |
| FR-GOV-15 | The system shall require the approver of a promotion to be a different principal from the requester. | B14 tab 1 | MUST | A requester approving their own promotion is refused. **[ASSUMPTION]** — the wireframe shows requester and approval separately but does not state the rule; separation of duties is applied consistently with [`iam.md`](./iam.md) FR-IAM-11. |
| FR-GOV-16 | The system shall record in the audit log every configuration change, agent publish, permission grant and data export, naming the actor, the action, the environment and the timestamp. | B14 tab 2 `[rule]` | MUST | Each of the four categories produces an entry. The five seeded entries render. An action in any of the four categories with no corresponding entry is a defect, verified by an exhaustive test over the audited operation list. |
| FR-GOV-17 | The system shall make the audit log append-only, with no update or delete capability available to any role including `Super Admin`, enforced by database grant rather than by application logic. | B14 tab 2 `[rule]`; architecture §10 | MUST | No `UPDATE` or `DELETE` grant exists on the audit table for any application database user. An attempt through the application returns a method-not-supported error; an attempt through the database user fails at the database. |
| FR-GOV-18 | The system shall display per-dependency observability showing p95 latency, error rate and a derived health status. | B14 tab 3 | MUST | The four seeded dependencies render with all three columns. Figures come from OpenTelemetry data, not from stored constants. |
| FR-GOV-19 | The system shall derive a dependency's health status from measured latency and error rate against configured thresholds, reporting `Healthy` or `Degraded`. | B14 tab 3 | MUST | A dependency at 6.1% error rate reports `Degraded`; one at 0.2% reports `Healthy`. **[ASSUMPTION]** — the wireframe shows the statuses without thresholds; the thresholds are error rate above 5% or p95 above the dependency's configured budget. |
| FR-GOV-20 | The system shall link a degraded dependency to the configuration surface where its incident is handled. | B14 tab 3; [`cross-module.md`](./cross-module.md) | SHOULD | The degraded SEWA bill API row links to its circuit-breaker configuration, and that breaker is in the state the observability screen implies ([`tools.md`](./tools.md) FR-TOOL-22). |
| FR-GOV-21 | The system shall maintain a consent ledger per citizen recording each consent grant and withdrawal with its timestamp and scope. | B14 tab 4 | MUST | An opt-in and a subsequent opt-out both appear in the ledger with timestamps. Outbound send checks read the ledger ([`channels.md`](./channels.md) FR-CHAN-16). |
| FR-GOV-22 | The system shall honour an erasure request by removing the citizen's personal data from all four stores and shall report completion per store. | B14 tab 4 | MUST | After erasure, the citizen's transcripts, derived memory, vectors, graph attributions and cache entries are absent, and the report names all four stores. Records retained under statutory obligation are listed as retained with the obligation cited (FR-PAY-08). |
| FR-GOV-23 | The system shall configure transcript retention from 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or 7 years, defaulting to 90 days, applying to transcripts and derived memory. | B14 tab 4 | MUST | All four options are selectable, defaulting to 90 days. Transcripts and derived memory beyond the period are absent after the purge job runs. **This is now the single retention authority** — see [`risks.md`](./risks.md) RISK-022 and [`verification.md`](./verification.md) FR-VERI-13. |
| FR-GOV-24 | The system shall retain transaction records for the statutory seven-year period irrespective of the transcript retention setting. | B14 tab 4 `[rule]` | MUST | The carve-out is enforced in the purge job's scope, not by a configured exception a user could remove. Asserted by setting retention to 30 days and verifying a 6-month-old transaction is intact while its transcript is gone. |
| FR-GOV-25 | The system shall configure data residency from `UAE — Sharjah DC`, `UAE — Dubai DC` and `Region-flexible`, defaulting to the Sharjah data centre. | B14 tab 4 | MUST | All three options persist. The setting is recorded per tenant and is visible in the privacy screen. |
| FR-GOV-26 | The system shall enumerate, for the configured residency setting, every processing location for citizen text — including third-party model, embedding and reranking providers — and shall surface any conflict between the configured residency and the actual processing locations. | B14 tab 4; ADR-0004 | MUST | With residency set to the Sharjah data centre and cloud model providers configured, the screen reports the conflict naming each out-of-region provider and capability. The system does not report compliance it cannot deliver. Directly related to [`risks.md`](./risks.md) RISK-001. |
| FR-GOV-27 | The system shall enforce retention through a scheduled purge job whose runs are recorded with their scope, the record counts removed and their outcome. | B14 tab 4 | MUST | Each run produces a record. A failed run is retried and reported, never silently skipped. A retention setting with no successful purge run in its period is an alertable condition. |
| FR-GOV-28 | The system shall apply retention, erasure and residency settings per tenant, and one tenant's setting shall never govern another tenant's data. | ADR-0002; B14 tab 4 | MUST | Tenant A at 30-day retention and tenant B at 1 year purge independently. Covered by the isolation gate in [`tenant-isolation.md`](./tenant-isolation.md). |
| FR-GOV-29 | Approving or rejecting a promotion and writing its audit entry shall be one atomic operation, so no decision can exist without its audit entry and no audit entry without its decision. | `governance`; B14 tab 1 → tab 2 | MUST | Approve the seeded promotion: the audit entry is present on the next read. Inject an audit-write failure: the decision is rolled back and the promotion remains pending. Counts of decisions and their audit entries are equal at all times. *(Cross-module invariant; also listed in [`cross-module.md`](./cross-module.md).)* |
