# 5.15 `analytics` — command centre, explorer, feedback loop (B1)

| ID | Requirement | Source | Pri | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-ANLY-01 | The system shall provide a date-range selector of `Today`, `Last 7 days` and `Last 30 days` that re-renders every panel on the overview. | B1 tab 1 | MUST | Switching range changes the KPI figures, the channel-split chart and the top-intent counts together. No panel retains a figure from the previous range. |
| FR-ANLY-02 | The system shall report conversation volume, containment rate, deflection rate and tool error rate for the selected range and tenant. | B1 tab 1 | MUST | All four KPIs render for all three ranges. Each figure is computed from conversation and trace records for the tenant, and reconciles with a direct query over the same period. |
| FR-ANLY-03 | The system shall report the channel split for the selected range. | B1 tab 1 | MUST | The chart redraws per range and its series sum equals the conversation volume KPI for the same range. |
| FR-ANLY-04 | The system shall report the top intents with their counts for the selected range. | B1 tab 1 | MUST | Intents are derived from recorded routing decisions, not from a static list. Counts scale with the selected range. |
| FR-ANLY-05 | The system shall provide a conversation explorer filterable by `All`, `Escalated`, `Resolved` and `Abandoned`. | B1 tab 2 | MUST | Each filter returns only conversations with that outcome ([`conversation.md`](./conversation.md) FR-CONV-14). The filter is applied server-side, so paging is consistent. |
| FR-ANLY-06 | The explorer shall show per conversation the citizen, channel, intent, outcome, rating and recency. | B1 tab 2 | MUST | All six columns render. The citizen identifier respects PII masking; an unverified citizen is not given a fabricated name. |
| FR-ANLY-07 | The system shall expand a conversation inline to show its transcript with a footer stating the outcome, the rating and that PII has been redacted. | B1 tab 2 | MUST | The transcript shown is the stored, masked transcript. The footer states the redaction rather than implying the raw text was available. |
| FR-ANLY-08 | The system shall export conversation transcripts, and every export shall be recorded in the audit log with the actor and the record count. | B1 tab 2; B14 tab 2 | MUST | Exporting produces a file of masked transcripts scoped to the requesting tenant, and an audit entry of the form "Exported N conversation transcripts". Export requires an explicit permission. |
| FR-ANLY-09 | The system shall maintain a thumbs-down review queue in which each item carries a volume and a root-cause tag and can be marked fixed or reopened. | B1 tab 3 | MUST | The three seeded items render with volumes and root causes. Marking fixed removes the item; reopening restores it. Both transitions are recorded with actor and timestamp. |
| FR-ANLY-10 | The system shall cluster unanswered questions by frequency and shall offer at least two resolution paths per cluster, each of which removes the item from the queue. | B1 tab 3 | MUST | The three seeded clusters render with their frequencies. Either resolution dequeues the item and records which path was taken. |
| FR-ANLY-11 | The system shall make each resolution path create the corresponding artefact: a knowledge entry in `knowledge`, or a flow in `flows`. | B1 tab 3 `[rule]` | MUST | Resolving as a knowledge gap creates a draft knowledge item attributed to the cluster; resolving as a flow gap creates a draft flow. The created artefact links back to the originating cluster, so the improvement loop is traceable in both directions. |
| FR-ANLY-12 | The system shall publish the definition of every reported metric alongside the figure, including the distinction between containment and deflection. | B1 tab 1 | MUST | Each KPI exposes its definition and window. **[ASSUMPTION]** — the wireframe defines neither term; containment is the share of conversations resolved without a human, and deflection is the share that would otherwise have become a channel contact. Deflection is therefore a subset of containment, matching the seeded figures where deflection is always the lower number. See [`risks.md`](./risks.md) RISK-008. |
| FR-ANLY-13 | The system shall compute platform-wide figures through the audited cross-tenant rollup path and shall never aggregate across tenants inside a feature module. | ADR-0002 rule 5; [`platform.md`](./platform.md) FR-PLAT-08 | MUST | A `Platform`-scoped dashboard shows cross-tenant totals produced by the rollup path, with an audit entry. No analytics query in a feature module spans more than one tenant. |
